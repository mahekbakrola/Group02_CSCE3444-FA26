## Original design judgments

Evidence matching is a transparent heuristic rather than a psychometric test. Technical skills are detected only from confirmed, scoped work evidence, with self-reported skills shown separately. Qualitative results are accepted. Source existence is not semantic proof: users must review every claim. The question bank is designed around omissions (projects, labs, awards, responsibilities, tools, scale, context and individual contribution) and has separate first-year, unrelated-work, multiple-project, career-target, existing-résumé and specific-job flows. Recommendations and project roadmaps are authored suggestions, not occupational certification requirements.

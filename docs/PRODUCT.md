# Folio product requirements

## Source proposal, read in full

The three-page Project proposal.pdf (provided September 9, 2026) contains a typed résumé-building proposal and two handwritten additions. Page 1 requires interviewing students, generating editable professional résumés, and downloading them. Page 2 adds comprehensive interviews, personalization, job research, sorting and recommendations, and answer editing. Page 3 adds first-year engineering career exploration, role requirements, finding suitable fields, and links for further help. Folio unifies these as an evidence-led career workspace. The proposed personality test is replaced with explicitly heuristic interest, preference, and evidence matching.

## Primary flow

Choose the fictional demonstration or start your own profile → brief onboarding → one adaptive question at a time → discover overlooked experience → review the master facts → create and approve résumé bullets → choose one of three single-column templates → export text-based PDF → compare a pasted job description → save a tailored version → explore career tasks and gaps.

## Product decisions

- Contact name, usable email, education, target, and one concrete contribution form the minimum draft. No employment requirement.
- Stronger information adds context, tools, individual contribution, and an outcome. Numbers are optional and must be confirmed with units/timeframe; no invented metrics.
- Course labs, hobbies, clubs, competitions, repair, tutoring, family responsibilities, research, open source, volunteering, and content creation are discovery entry points.
- Skills need an experience/project example. A skill list alone is self-reported. Repetition is not proof; neither are confidence labels.
- Career fit uses evidence overlap, interests, task enjoyment, and preferences. This is an inspectable heuristic, not a probability or a validated assessment. Occupational mappings are sourced; learning projects are Folio-authored suggestions.
- Master facts and résumé presentation are separate. Source edits invalidate bullet approval. Edits, deletions, rejection, reordering, duplication, regeneration, and restoration require explicit actions.
- Unknown and skipped answers remain unknown. Conditional questions are recalculated after edits. Multiple experiences use the same schema with independent entity scopes.
- Only final approved, current-source bullets can export. Imported résumé text remains unverified until confirmed.
- No age, gender, citizenship, immigration, health, street address, or other unnecessary sensitive data is requested. Job eligibility is not inferred.
- No invented live jobs. Seeded opportunities are visibly labeled fictional; a pasted description is user-provided. Links go to authoritative occupation sources.

## Delivery scope

Core includes authenticated per-user database storage, onboarding, branching question bank, experience discovery, repeatable experiences/projects, master facts, provenance, résumé versions and history, three templates and PDF download, job comparison and tailoring, career exploration, dashboard, privacy controls and responsive dark/light UI. Optional external AI and O*NET providers require credentials. Local/demo deterministic behavior is labeled honestly. A selectable 3D career graph has an accessible list equivalent.

## Design

Charcoal workspace, pale mint actions, teal orbital evidence graph, warm amber findings, editorial serif accents alongside readable sans-serif text. Compact navigation, generous working space, a visible next action, and no oversized marketing barrier. Résumé exports remain white, single-column, and conventional.
